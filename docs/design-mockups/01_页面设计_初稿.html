Research

Effector genomics in oomycete pathogens

Comparative and population genomics of effector repertoires in Phytophthora sojae, combining long-read assembly with isolate-level resequencing to separate conserved core effectors from rapidly evolving accessory ones.

effector · Phytophthora sojae · pangenome

Host resistance in wild soybean

Mapping resistance to Phytophthora root and stem rot in Glycine soja populations, with BSA, CIM linkage mapping and GWAS on the same material.

Glycine soja · GWAS · QTL
